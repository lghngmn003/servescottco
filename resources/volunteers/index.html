ReadyNow!

Emergency alert app for ICE encounters — by Human Rights First

ReadyNow! is a free, secure emergency app that lets you notify trusted contacts and connect with no-cost legal support with a single button press. If you or someone you know faces an ICE encounter, this app ensures your network is alerted immediately and legal aid is on the way.

  • Creates an emergency plan with contacts for childcare, legal defense, and personal safety
  • One-tap alert notifies your pre-set contacts and connects you to the Acacia Center for Justice legal network
  • All data is encrypted and stored locally — wiped after an alert is sent so it can't be accessed if your phone is seized
  • Available in English, Spanish, Korean, Mandarin, Haitian Kreyol, and French

Signal Security Settings

Configure Signal to protect your messages and your network

Signal is the most secure mainstream messaging app available — but only if it's configured correctly. As a ServeScott volunteer, please take 15 minutes to complete these settings. They protect not just you, but every contact and group you're part of.

  • Hide your identity: Use a username instead of your phone number, remove your real name and photo
  • Enable disappearing messages: Set to 1 week for standard organizing, 1 day or less for sensitive actions
  • Secure notifications: Hide message content and sender name from your lock screen
  • Before any action: Leave AND delete Signal groups — a confiscated phone shouldn't expose your whole network
  • Set a strong passcode: 8+ random digits. Do not use biometric unlock (police can take it)
